The Assistant button in the header now opens a panel on the right of every page instead of a floating chat. On a wide screen it gets its own column you can resize, and on a phone it takes the whole screen. Opened next to a run, a test or an analysis, it adds a chip, so your next message is about what you are looking at.

When a batch of tests finishes, the chat shows one card with the passed and failed counts and the tests, each one click from Runs. Explain failures asks for an AI summary when you want one. A run you started that finishes while the panel is closed shows a short notice, and a dot marks new messages for you.
