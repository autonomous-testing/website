Every plan can now be paid monthly or once a year. Accounts that have never bought a plan are offered the first month on us. Checkout stays in euros, takes a billing address and a VAT ID for EU businesses, and reports a promotion code that does not apply instead of dropping it.

If you already pay, moving to a higher plan or to yearly billing happens in the app: you see the prorated amount, confirm it, and your existing subscription changes. A failed payment leaves your plan as it was.

The upgrade page now shows what every plan includes, a feature comparison and answers to common questions. When you reach a limit, the dialog says which limit, when it resets and how far your project has got, and the usage meter in the header shows the steps you have left.
