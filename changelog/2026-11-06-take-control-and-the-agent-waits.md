Take control used to send the agent a message it could ignore. Now the agent finishes its current action, pauses and hands you the live browser, framed in yellow while it is yours. Click Hand back to agent and it carries on from the page as you left it.

One switch does this in the Live panel and in the run dialog. When the agent asks for help, the reply box and Resume stay in view, and the status and the tab title say "Waiting for you" until you answer.
