# Privacy notice for the free tutorials

This notice covers [wopee.io/learn](/learn/). The controller is wopee labs s.r.o., Prague, Czech Republic.

## What we collect, and why

- **Email address**, to tell you when a tutorial opens and, if you agree, to send you Wopee.io news.
- **Name and GitHub username**, to issue your certificate and its verify link.
- **Team details** (optional): team size and what your team wants to learn, to plan the team material.

## Who processes it

HubSpot (forms and contact records) and Brevo (sending email).

## How long we keep it

Emails: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data. Certificates: as long as the verify link exists, or until you ask us to delete them.

## The lab

The lab check sends nothing to us unless you opt in with `LEARN_SHARE=1`.

## Contact

Write to [help@wopee.io](mailto:help@wopee.io) to see, correct or delete your data. Your other rights: see our [GDPR page](/gdpr/).
