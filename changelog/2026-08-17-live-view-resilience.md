The live browser picture now survives closing and reopening the viewer or switching panes, and a reconnecting viewer gets the last frame immediately, labeled as a replay until the live stream resumes. After a crawl finishes, the Live stage shows per-section generation progress instead of going blank. And run times across the app now display in your timezone, not the server's.
