On a phone, Runs shows one pane at a time: the run list, the steps or the comparison, and the back button walks between them. On a tablet, a run picker sits above the steps with the comparison beside it. An analysis shows one column with a scenario detail, and Project Settings opens full screen with a category picker.

The tab row fits without scrolling: Analysis, Scenarios and Runs stay, and the rest moves under More. Tables size their columns to the content and switch to compact rows with a menu on small screens.
