title: "Pay once, land straight back in your projects"
description: "After checkout you return to Wopee.io, your plan is active on arrival and you continue to Projects, even when the payment confirmation is slow to arrive."
---

Upgrading used to leave you on the payment provider's thank-you page, and coming back could send you to the upgrade screen again until the payment confirmation caught up, so a successful payment looked like it had failed.

Checkout now returns you to a confirmation page in Wopee.io. It activates your plan as soon as the payment is confirmed, without waiting for the background notification, welcomes you and takes you on to Projects. A promotion code that does not apply to your order is dropped instead of blocking the payment.
