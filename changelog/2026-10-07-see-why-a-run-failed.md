title: "See why a run failed, not just that it did"
description: "A failed run or crawl now says why, from an unreachable URL to a failed login, and any run's Playwright trace opens in Trace Viewer from the Logs menu."
---

A run that stops early now records the reason: the app URL could not be reached, the URL is invalid, or the login failed, with a one-line detail. A failed crawl opens with a callout that explains what went wrong and what to do next, with Re-run crawl, View logs and links to Project settings and Variables & Knowledge. A failed or stopped initial crawl can be re-run in place.

Each run's Playwright trace is saved when the run reports. Download it, or open it straight in Trace Viewer, from the Logs menu.
